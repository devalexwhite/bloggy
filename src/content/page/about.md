### Who is That Alex Guy?

<img src="/avatar.webp" height="200" class="mb-8 rounded" />


I live in Columbus, Ohio and am a dad of 2 kids, 2 dogs and a cat. I've been making stuff on computers since the early 2000s,  beginning with the Atari 400 (way after their prime), which led to writing C on Palm Pilots and finally coding on Mac.

These days I work in management, but still enjoy coding websites and apps by hand in my (limited) free time. I'm also an avid cyclists, hiker and enjoyer of retro video games.

I run a small consulting company called [Darn Fine Software](https://darnfinesoftware.com) and the [Columbus Vintage Computing Club](https://cbusvintagecomputing.org).

### What's this site for?

I use this space to write about things that interest and to post photos, projects and other things I tinker with. You'll mostly find content related to technology (vintage and modern), programming, cycling and nature.
