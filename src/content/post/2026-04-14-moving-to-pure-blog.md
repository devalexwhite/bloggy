Awhile back I built [mdServer](https://github.com/devalexwhite/md-server), a
markdown based blogging engine. It was fun to build, but I’ve found I
want to focus on other projects (and writing). With that, I’ve decided
to move to [Pure Blog](https://pureblog.org), the excellent
blogging platform created by [Kev
Quirk](https://kevquirk.com/).

I choose Pure Blog for ease of setup and customization ability. I
spun up a new site on Laravel Forge and dropped the files in via SFTP.
Easy-peezy! I’ll continue tweaking the CSS (currently inspired by the
lovely simple look of [Kia Kamgar’s
site](https://kiakamgar.com/posts)) and add more pages in the future.

I’ve created an nginx redirect that should (🤞) keep the old RSS feed
URL working, let me know if it broke!
